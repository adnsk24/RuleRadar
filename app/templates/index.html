RuleRadar AI — User Guide & Architecture

RuleRadar AI is a self-contained, enterprise document intelligence workstation engineered to analyze contracts, extract obligations, detect risk patterns, and compare document revisions without relying on paid cloud APIs.

Key Capabilities:

  • 20-Category Clause Segmentation: Identifies termination, indemnities, penalties, non-competes, and deposit terms.
  • Transparent Risk Engine: Evaluates unilateral clauses, disproportionate liquidated damages, and forfeiture rights with verbatim quotes and suggested legal questions.
  • Semantic Version Comparison: Matches clauses across revisions to detect shifted deadlines, adjusted monetary amounts, and modified obligations.
  • Evidence-Grounded Q&A: Extracts verbatim answers citing exact page numbers and clauses with zero generative hallucination.

Privacy & Deployment:

Execution runs 100% on local CPU. All models and vectors are cached in local memory, guaranteeing confidential processing.
